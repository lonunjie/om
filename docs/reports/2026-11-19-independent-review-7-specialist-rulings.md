## Evidence and negative tests

The prototype fixtures record the open cases deliberately. The Rust fixture `kits2_04_valid_max_hostname_exposes_existing_kit_size_limit` asserts `kit.len() > MAX_KIT_BYTES`, and the JSON supplies `certified_kit_length = 4342`. The `rk2a_03` test asserts that a new properly signed request with a different nonce still classifies as `OlderSheet` for an unchanged recovery child. The `rk2a` fixture's checkpoint classifier accepts only generation-1 checkpoints and records its known limits in comments. `CC3A-02` has no parent key-rollover positive yet. These are open cases for the new vectors, not passing implementations of the general cases, and the independent parser and generator evidence stays labelled incomplete until the new cases are written and run.

New vector namespaces are reserved as proposals (the repository-wide collision check at import found none): `CC3B-01..03`, `KITS3-01..04`, `RK2B-01..04`, `RK2C-01..04`. The proposal documents specify each. Keep old goldens as historical controls; do not silently relabel an old passing fixture to claim the new security property. Byte-exact new fixture strings and real signatures are **not supplied by this review**; the CDDL and numerical registries are **not yet edited**.

## Metadata and authority assessment

- **Historical credential carriage** lets a selected controller see past public signer keys and correlate rotations. It gives neither the origin nor the old credential new authority.
- **The larger certified kit** stays secret within authenticated same-account channels and DeviceVault but may reveal new message-size/timing buckets to a Home and increases local resource/DoS pressure. The optional promotion shortcut cannot bypass place-certificate checking.
- **Policy history** remains under the existing RecoveryRecord AEAD and four size buckets. A stolen root/kit reveals more account-policy history; the Home only sees bucket changes. There is no verifier-time evidence in the record.
- **The critical request-digest extension creates publicly correlatable metadata** between any disclosed ACS child and an observed recovery-request digest. The Home may learn the request digest as part of its inbox/board operation. A specialist must judge this against the audited metadata floor; if rejected, the extension must **not** be enabled and the original-request classification remains `needs_evidence`, not “any request”.

**Where authority remains:** the independently owner-pinned checkpoint for sheet identity, existing W0 parent constitutional rules for every subsequent transition, the parent-committed recovery method for request and recovery signatures, verifier-local interval and original request-specific veto evidence for owner-recovery completion, and durable admission for kit distribution. No Home, encrypted record, sibling message or QR metadata becomes a vote, timer or account-authority oracle.
