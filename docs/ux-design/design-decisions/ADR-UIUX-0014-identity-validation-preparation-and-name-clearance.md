# ADR-UIUX-0014 — Identity validation preparation and name-clearance boundary

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-29

## Context

Revision 11C produced three materially different identity hypotheses but no human evidence or legal clearance. Public-web research in R12 also found existing messaging/software uses around `Open Messenger`, `OpenMessenger`, `OMessenger`, `OneMessenger`, and active software/service uses of the internal candidate labels `One Thread`, `Open Loop`, and `Peer Link`.

## Decision

1. Keep **One Thread**, **Open Loop**, and **Peer Link** as visual shortlist hypotheses only.
2. Human testing hides internal candidate names and rationale until the end.
3. Do not freeze a final logo in R12.
4. Do not treat any OM long-form expansion as legally cleared.
5. Require formal trademark/name/visual-similarity review before public identity freeze.
6. Preserve candidate geometry during the first human-test round so results remain comparable.
7. Use synthetic platform grids only for test preparation/static review; physical-device launcher evidence remains required.
8. Functional icons remain a separate semantic system; identity testing does not reopen the icon constitution.

## Consequences

OM can gather useful identity evidence without prematurely turning a design-study label into a brand name. The project also surfaces naming risk early enough that a visual identity decision is not accidentally coupled to an unavailable word mark.
