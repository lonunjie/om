# Preliminary identity/name collision research — Revision 12

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Evidence class:** public-web reconnaissance only. **Not a legal opinion, trademark clearance, freedom-to-operate determination, or global registry search.** A qualified trademark professional must perform formal clearance before public launch.

## Important distinction

`One Thread`, `Open Loop`, and `Peer Link` are internal names for visual design hypotheses. They are **not proposed product names**. Their name collisions therefore do not by themselves reject the visual shapes, but they reinforce the need to keep user studies blinded and to avoid turning internal candidate labels into public branding.

## Product-name collision signals

### OpenMessenger / Open Messenger

Public search finds multiple prior uses directly in messaging/software:

- Apple's App Store still exposes an **Open messenger** social-networking app by GDVerse SRL (originally released in 2015; listing remains accessible): https://apps.apple.com/gm/app/open-messenger/id968379871
- SourceForge has an **Open Messenger** GNU/MSN messenger project registered in 2001: https://sourceforge.net/projects/gnumsg/
- SourceForge also has a separate **OpenMessenger** project registered in 2008: https://sourceforge.net/projects/openmessenger/
- An `open-messenger` / `open-messenger-ui` software package is indexed by Skypack: https://www.skypack.dev/view/open-messenger

**Design consequence:** `OpenMessenger` is descriptively attractive but not collision-free. Do not assume the long product name is legally available merely because OM's architecture is new.

### OMessenger / OM Messenger

There is an established **OMessenger** cross-platform office messaging product from Srimax, with current public web presence and store listings. Google Play reports 100K+ downloads, and Apple's App Store still lists the app.

- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=srimax.outlookmessenger
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/omessenger/id582993443
- https://omessenger.com/

**Design consequence:** the compact spoken/written form “O Messenger” / “OMessenger” has a strong practical collision signal in the exact messaging category. Public naming work must treat this as material.

### OneMessenger

Public search finds active/recent `OneMessenger` uses, including a Business Central collaboration product and an announced OneSystem product family.

- https://penduline.de/onemessenger.html (referenced by current public posts)
- https://www.onetube.app/ (lists OneMessenger in its product family)

**Design consequence:** do not assume the “OneMessenger” expansion is globally ownable as a product name. It can remain part of internal philosophy unless formal clearance says otherwise.

## Internal candidate-label collision signals

- Public USPTO-derived records show **ONE THREAD** filings/registration in software R&D / software-service contexts, including serial 99746652 / registration 8438388. Example secondary record: https://trademarks.justia.com/997/46/one-99746652.html

Example records: https://trademarks.justia.com/777/68/openloop-77768290.html and https://trademarks.justia.com/791/44/openloop-79144143.html

### Peer Link

- `PeerLink` is actively used by multiple current technology/social projects.
- A live/renewed U.S. **PEERLINK** registration is publicly indexed for downloadable file collaboration/sharing/replication software (registration 3652414): https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-detail/77505089/PEERLINK
- `PeerLink` also exists as a P2P networking library and other products.

## Visual-motif crowding signals

Text/image search also reinforces the static design review:

- circular-loop marks are common across technology and healthcare;
- interlocking rings/links are a crowded “connection/network” motif;
- `om`/letter ligatures are common as a general monogram class, but the specific One Thread silhouette is less dependent on a generic enclosing circle.

This reconnaissance is **not enough to approve or reject a visual mark**. Formal visual-similarity clearance requires jurisdiction/class scope, relevant earlier rights, marketplace context, and counsel.

## R12 naming rule

1. Human identity testing uses **Candidate A/B/C**, not `One Thread`, `Open Loop`, or `Peer Link`.
2. Do not publicly present `OpenMessenger`, `OMessenger`, `OneMessenger`, or any other expansion as cleared.
3. The short `OM` identity and final wordmark remain provisional until formal naming/trademark review.
4. A visual winner may be selected for product testing while the public word mark remains unresolved, provided engineering treats both as provisional.
