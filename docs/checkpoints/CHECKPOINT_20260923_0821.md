## A-013 — metadata threat model

Resolved in `METADATA_THREAT_MODEL.md` and ADR-0098.

Key decisions:

- metadata privacy is surface-specific, not implied by E2EE;
- AccountID/ConversationID/SpaceID and inner MLS/group identifiers are not normal provider-routing addresses;
- relay routing uses provider-scoped opaque capabilities and encrypted independently randomized outer transport envelopes;
- privacy UI/provider contracts distinguish IP/timing/size/membership/push/access-pattern exposure from content confidentiality;
- Recommended/Hardened/Maximum Privacy may progressively add bounded padding, private discovery, provider separation, relayed calls, batching and anonymity transports;
- no claim of universal traffic-analysis resistance/global-observer anonymity is made.

V-015 retains the packet/provider-trace and privacy-claim proof obligation.

## A-014 — decentralized abuse/moderation

Resolved in `DECENTRALIZED_ABUSE_MODERATION.md` and ADR-0099.

Key decisions:

- user-local, relationship, Room/Space and provider enforcement remain separate scoped authorities;
- `ModerationDecision` records authenticated scoped action without automatically exposing private report evidence;
- optional signed `SafetyAdvisory` sources let communities/users share abuse signals, but subscribers decide enforcement;
- no universal OM moderator, mandatory labeler, global reputation score or master blocklist exists;
- non-public safety subjects should use labeler-scoped pseudonymous references where feasible;
- provider denial/delisting affects provider custody/reach, not AccountID/Room/Service authority;
- permissionless identity means OM does not promise perfect permanent human-level blocking across Sybil identities; IntroGate/admission/resource controls constrain unsolicited reach instead.

V-016 retains formal/adversarial proof of authorization, Sybil/resource, advisory and provider-boundary behavior.

## A-015 — 20-year protocol longevity / 0M continuity

Resolved in `PROTOCOL_LONGEVITY_AND_0M_CONTINUITY.md` and ADR-0100.

Key decisions:

- every published protocol generation/profile is independently preservable/interpretable through a mirrorable `Protocol Continuity Bundle`;
- existing registry/profile IDs never change meaning and are never reused incompatibly;
- protocol generations/forks coexist explicitly and critical unknown semantics fail safely;
- a live OM Project server/registry is not a runtime validity oracle;
- dominant clients/extensions gain no special protocol authority from market share;
- proprietary/permission-gated extensions cannot become mandatory baseline OM conformance;
- crypto/project/provider failure uses authenticated migration where continuity can be proved, otherwise explicit re-verification/successor/fork semantics;
- no emergency project master key may seize user identity after catastrophic failure.

V-017 retains the creator/forge/domain/provider/release-root continuity drill and multi-generation/fork proof obligation.
