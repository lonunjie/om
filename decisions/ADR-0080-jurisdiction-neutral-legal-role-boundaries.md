# ADR-0080 — Legal exposure is jurisdiction-neutral and role-scoped

## Status
Accepted — 2026-09-23

## Decision
No country is OM's protocol home jurisdiction. The project minimizes avoidable exposure by operating as a source/spec/release steward rather than a mandatory network/content/payment intermediary. Any project-controlled hosted service or distribution receives role-specific review for the jurisdictions in which that actor offers it.

Standard open-source warranty/liability terms remain intact. Optional providers, organizations, users and distributors bear the obligations created by roles they actually choose to operate, subject to mandatory law.

## Consequence
Country-specific legal questions are removed from canonical protocol architecture. This is not a claim of legal immunity.
