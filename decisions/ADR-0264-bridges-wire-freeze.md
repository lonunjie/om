# ADR-0264 — W4 `bridges` canonical wire freeze and adapter-contract design review

- **Status:** Delegated design decision — repo-local W4 wire freeze and **AI design review only**; **independent review remains required**.
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Scope:** W4 `bridges` wire objects 53–58, typed `ConversationEvent` key 14 and their canonical vectors. No change to W0, W1, W2 or W3 receiver rules or to W4 services key 13.
- **Precondition:** ADR-0260 **already authorized engineering**, waiving only the V-013 pre-engineering gate. V-013 pre-release independent contract review, two independently written codecs, implementation and multi-bridge evidence are still open. This decision **does not authorize release** or turn the feature on.
- **Authority:** This is a **delegated design construction**, **not** a change to accepted/audited architecture, not the operator's additional authorization, **not independent human review**, and not an independent second implementation.

## Decision

Freeze [`spec/cddl/bridge.cddl`](../spec/cddl/bridge.cddl) and add only the following W4 assignments to the **complete** existing registries; all prior assignments, including the services types 47–52 and `ConversationEvent` key 13, remain intact.

| Form | Type ID | Authentication / role | Body hash | Max top-level encoded bytes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| `BridgePrincipal` | 53 | None; unsigned identity wrapper | None; BridgeID is **random stable 32-byte** provider-independent ID | 256 |
| `BridgeControlState` | 54 | `SignedEnvelope` / `controller` (signature role 1) | `OM-BRIDGE-CONTROL-HASH-v1` | 65,536 |
| `BridgeMapping` | 55 | `SignedEnvelope` / `controller` (role 1) **plus separate actual Room approval** | `OM-BRIDGE-MAPPING-HASH-v1` | 32,768 |
| `InteropTrace` | 56 | None; untrusted/bridge-attested lineage wrapper | None; origin MAC is separate | 8,192 |
| `ForeignProvenance` | 57 | None; bridge attestation comes from authenticated event author, not this wrapper | None | 16,384 |
| `ForeignIdentityLink` | 58 | `SignedEnvelope` / eligible **Account controller** (role 1), plus independently checked foreign proof | `OM-FOREIGN-IDENTITY-LINK-HASH-v1` | 32,768 |

The registered signatures are `OM-BRIDGE-CONTROL-SIG-v1`, `OM-BRIDGE-MAPPING-SIG-v1` and `OM-FOREIGN-IDENTITY-LINK-SIG-v1`. The six signature/hash labels use the existing `DS-SIG`/`DS-HASH` canonical-body constructions and role 1, not a new signature algorithm or role. Register `OM-INTEROP-FP-MAC-v1` separately for Room-secret-keyed origin fingerprints (construction below). Domain labels are unique; no previous label is repurposed.

`InteropTrace` and `ForeignProvenance` have unambiguous standalone **top-level wrappers**: `{0:56,1:1,2:InteropTraceBody}` and `{0:57,1:1,2:ForeignProvenanceBody}`. The nested bodies are separate registered structs; their top-level wrappers do not get inserted inside `ConversationEvent`. **The event's existing type remains 10** and its reserved **key 14** becomes a direct `ForeignProvenanceBody`, a W4-only field. Key 13 remains `ServiceEventRepresentation` for services; key 13 and key 14 may **never coexist** in one event. No new `event_kind` number is required: a degraded foreign edit may use the existing `message.create` kind with an explicit fidelity flag. No fictitious author or native event kind is minted.

**Every bridge-imported event is cryptographically authored by `{0:3,1:BridgeID}`** (PrincipalRef kind 3) using an active authorized **bridge endpoint** as key 6. Never substitute an Account, Service or the asserted foreign actor as the event author. Its key 14 must include a fingerprint and trace, whose last traversed BridgeID matches the author; accepted imports cannot have an exhausted hop limit. Its key 11 must still validate against the already frozen corresponding event-kind schema, **including a W0-valid `message.create` body**. Later-W1 event kinds whose kind-specific schema is not provided are not newly accepted by this overlay. Display hints and optional identity links remain bridge-attested evidence, not native Account authentication.

**W0 behavior is mandatory and unchanged:** A W0 receiver **rejects** an event with key 14 as an unknown **critical** numeric field, just as it rejects key 13. It cannot silently drop either field, reinterpret provenance as Account authorship, claim it is an ordinary native message, or replace the rejection with acceptance. A sender must negotiate support or provide an explicitly separate W0-compatible fallback through existing ADR-0128 rule 18 rules. Merely parsing W4 data is not permission to use it in W0.

## Important normalization

1. **A bridge is only a bridge.** `BridgeID` and its endpoint keys are distinct from an AccountID, ServiceID, DeviceIdentity or foreign user ID. Control authority is a threshold of independently governed Account/organization/Space/Room controllers; a hosting provider, bridge, adapter or endpoint has no implicit vote, no AccountControlState vote and no automatic Room control capability. `BridgeControlState.authorization_evidence` is the authenticated `SignedEnvelope.signatures` plus **external verified controller authorization**, not duplicate inline signatures. Controllers, endpoints and adapters are individually unique.
2. **Full membership is indivisible.** Continuous private-Room bridging has only the **visible full MLS-member** construction (ADR-0129), not `selected_context`, selective encryption, a designated member-device forwarder or a Space backdoor. A bridge can decrypt *all content from its membership epochs*. `allowed_event_classes`, `direction` and `InteropCapabilities` constrain **export and translation only**, never its read/decryption powers. Room governance, `room.add_bridge` and any required human quorum, new MLS epoch, visible roster, disclosure of E2EE termination and foreign-leg security claim must be independently verified. **No bridge endpoint counts toward a human quorum**; nor does a bridge/service-derived Room role self-approve.
3. **Control vs Room approval.** A `BridgeMapping` controller signature and `approval_evidence` digest do not themselves admit the bridge, grant Room rights or prove a quorum. An accepted Room control transition under Room authorization, current BridgeControlState head, MLS admission/active membership interval and actual evidence contents are independent necessary inputs. A Room control revocation removes the endpoint via ordinary reducing-intent liveness (ADR-0128); no old foreign plaintext is reclaimed. One-shot manual forwarding uses the ordinary visible export path, not an undisclosed ongoing mapping.
4. **State is auditable and rollback resistant.** `BridgeControlState`, `BridgeMapping` and `ForeignIdentityLink` have separately numbered incarnation-preserving predecessor chains: version 1 has no `previous_*_hash`, every later version exactly one. Verify against the highest **authenticated** head; reject skipped versions, replays, forks, predecessor substitutions, resurrection of revoked links/mappings and revoked endpoint credentials. Keep revoked endpoint tombstones and prevent reactivation under a reused incarnation. Emergency removal must be rebased as a monotone reduction, not delayed by an unrelated stale/forked update; propagate new heads and remove MLS leaves from all bound Rooms.
5. **Room-keyed origin, not bridge- or public-Room-keyed origin.** `origin_fingerprint = HMAC-SHA256(room_origin_secret, OM-CBOR(["OM-INTEROP-FP-MAC-v1", origin_network_namespace, stable_foreign_origin_ref]))`. Input is a deterministic, length-delimited OM-CBOR array; the Room-specific secret is a **32-byte private value** every admitted bridge can derive from authenticated Room-only secret state. It is **not** a public Room ID, a BridgeID or a per-bridge key; it is never placed in `BridgeMapping`, `InteropTrace`, `ForeignProvenance`, `ConversationEvent`, a public registry object or a public transcript. The result is 32 bytes; the same origin imported through independent bridges in one Room deduplicates, and the same origin in distinct Rooms is not stably linkable by its fingerprint. Secret provisioning/rotation and derivation must undergo external security review; public-key or Room-ID fallback is forbidden.
6. **Trace is ordered lineage.** `traversed_bridge_ids` and `transformation_ids` are **ordered, unique arrays**; `hop_count == len(traversed_bridge_ids) <= hop_limit`, caps are enforced, any repeated BridgeID means a reflection, and a hop at or above the limit is not admitted as a new inbound event. Both the `ForeignProvenance.origin_fingerprint` and `InteropTraceBody.origin_fingerprint` must agree. The immediate observed foreign network can differ from the original network if relayed; **do not force their namespaces equal**. Room policy may veto reflection paths; cross-bridge dedup uses strong keyed provenance, never display names, timestamps or text similarity. Missing origin assurance is visibly marked `origin_unverified` and cannot be silently upgraded to verified identity.
7. **No hidden relaying or history.** Bridge-to-bridge re-export is **off by default**; turning on a mapping's `relay_bridged` is never sufficient without the Room's explicit approval and all foreign paths actually preserving trace metadata. No historical plaintext is exported from epochs before admission, from removed epochs or during revoked membership. A `ForeignIdentityLink` requires eligible native Account control-device authorization and a distinct validated foreign/bridge proof with exact namespace, actor, validity and revocation. A valid link changes **presentation only**, never author type or Account rights.
8. **Fidelity is mandatory evidence.** Supported feature declarations, target mapping, capability mismatch, `origin_unverified`, `foreign_actor_unverified`, loss of reply targets, edit/delete/reaction/poll degradation and independent external-leg security claims must be surfaced faithfully. An untracked target is not rebound to an unrelated event. External deletes are requests, not cryptographic erasure; unsupported polls are not silently mirrored with changed voter privacy. Outbound operations and dedup/echo state must survive restart for the supported retry horizon; unknown external-send outcomes require reconciliation or the foreign network's safe idempotency, not fresh duplicate operation IDs.

## Canonical validation rules

- Follow OM-CBOR-v1's shortest-form, definite-length, deterministic map ordering and numeric typed-map keys. Reject CBOR negative integers, tags, floats, non-shortest numbers, indefinite items, invalid UTF-8, duplicate/out-of-order map keys and trailing data. Reject `null` for typed fields. Enforce each registered `max_bytes` **before any decode** and each collection/value bound afterward.
- Reject all unassigned/reserved numeric fields and absent mandatory fields; optional fields are omitted, never CBOR null. Key 23 keeps the pre-existing extension rules: unknown noncritical extension entries are ignorable, whereas unknown critical entries or identifiers beginning `om.critical.` cause rejection. No bridge-specific extension identifier is allocated.
- Every registered `set` must be unique and strictly increasing in the **bytewise full canonical encoded element** order. Do not repair or normalize unsorted received data. Trace path and transformation arrays, by contrast, retain actual traversal order and are checked separately for duplicate IDs.
- A SignedEnvelope includes 1–16 deterministic-order, distinct-signer structurally valid signatures of the declared role. For `BridgeControlState`, the number of distinct signers cannot be less than the declared threshold; **that structural count is insufficient** for actual authorization. Verify controller identities against the predecessor authenticated control set and its threshold and other governance rules before acceptance. An unsigned wrapper proves no actor identity by itself.
- Versioned state and mapping/link chains require exact predecessor digest under their respective registered label, accepted predecessor, immutable identity/incarnation fields, live signer authorization and monotone revocation. `BridgeMapping` must reference the accepted BridgeControlState head. Its adapter namespace and allowed declared semantic features must match an actual authorized adapter, and outbound/relay operation requires metadata-preserving capability. A non-relay, inbound-only metadata-stripped path can be considered only under explicit Room policy and `origin_unverified` handling.
- A bridge-authored event without key 14 rejects; native Account or Service authorship combined with key 14 rejects, as do simultaneous keys 13 and 14. For imports verify active bridge endpoint, authorized Room binding, inbound direction, matching foreign scope, fresh MLS membership epoch, complete trace/fingerprint consistency, dedup, hop/reflection and the inherited kind-specific event-body validation. These checks require actual external authoritative state beyond CBOR.
- For `ForeignIdentityLink`, proof method, optional attesting BridgeID, signed Account authorization, exact AccountControlState head, expiry, evidence retrieval and lifecycle are separate requirements. Link issuance cannot rely on a foreign display hint, bridge signature alone, an untrusted proof URL or a purported AccountID from the bridge. Only a current, unrevoked, unexpired, genuinely verified link may inform UI.
- Never elevate a Room mapping, adaptation capability or bridge endpoint to an Account authority, full cross-network E2EE, native signature, a lower crypto floor, human quorum vote or tool/egress authorization. Do not accept a call-media participant merely because it has Room MLS epoch access (ADR-0128 rule 14).

## Evidence

- [`spec/cddl/bridge.cddl`](../spec/cddl/bridge.cddl), **complete** augmented registries and `verification/prototypes/bridges-wire/` supply **repo-local** one-codec evidence. The independent-of-CBOR-packages standard-library Python implementation encodes and decodes deterministic OM-CBOR-v1, validates numeric registry schemas and bridge-specific cross-field/chain constraints, and replays **27 positive and 98 negative** exact-wire vectors with exact expected error classes. Vectors include W0 mandatory key-14 rejection, preservation of the key-13 W0 rejection, wrong author/role, missing/unknown/null fields, map and set canonicality, signature roles, mapping/head consistency, Room-keyed fingerprint separation, loop exhaustion, chain forks, rollback, tombstone resurrection and identity-link expiry.
- [`NOTES.md`](../crates/om-archive-reader/NOTES.md) documents the candidate adapter contract **AI-only** review, with prioritized findings and recommended fixes. This does not certify the candidate's V-013 independent-review criterion. Nor are deterministic structural placeholder signatures actual cryptographic signatures. No supplied real adapter, MLS runtime, independent second codec, foreign proof provider or policy implementation was exercised.
- **Release remains prohibited** until V-013 pre-release obligations are met: independent contract/wire/security review by an eligible reviewer, second independently written codec comparing vectors and implementations, real adapter/multi-bridge tests, durable echo/dedup/retry behavior, full Room/MLS governance/consent and metadata-privacy evaluation, and separate release authorization. ADR-0260's engineering authorization remains exactly as it was; no release declaration is edited.

## Non-goals

No new event kind, change to W0 event or extension handling, modification to earlier-wave signed objects or services type 47–52, Account identity minting, bridge-to-account representation, hidden or narrower-than-full bridge membership, global provider registry, project bridge fleet, bypass of Room governance, real MLS control transition, approved HMAC secret-distribution protocol, foreign API implementation, cryptographic signature verification, proof-of-link verification or independent review. The wire freeze alone cannot prove confidentiality on the foreign leg or safety of a real adapter.

## Choices where the design is silent

The source architecture and logical schemas do not assign bridge numeric CBOR fields or values. Each following choice is a **stricter/simple delegated construction**, not a new audited grant or a relaxation of an accepted rule:

1. **Types, signatures and sizes:** consecutive IDs 53–58; 256/65,536/32,768/8,192/16,384/32,768 byte caps. Principal/trace/provenance wrappers are unsigned; controller control, mapping and native Account-authorized link are signed using only existing role 1. Only control/mapping/link receive DS-HASH identity/chain references.
2. **Controller/endpoint/adapters:** controllers have kind 1 Account, 2 organization, 3 Space governance, 4 Room governance and a 32-byte authority ID (1–32 controllers, threshold 1–32); endpoints have 16–32 byte incarnation IDs, active/quarantined/revoked states, public key bound 32–4,096, profile 1–65,535 and optional nonauthoritative host ID. At most 128 endpoints and 64 adapter definitions (one adapter ID and one namespace each). Bridge keys are never eligible Account devices. Adapter declarations are bounded feature enums, global and optional per-semantic size/count caps, `carries_interop_metadata`, optional separate external-E2EE claim and proof methods.
3. **Mapping scope and approval:** W4 continuous mapping uses Room scope `{0:2,1:ConversationID bstr32}` only; personal one-shot exports are separate. Mapping identity is a 16-byte incarnation plus immutable BridgeID, Room, foreign namespace and foreign scope. `approval_evidence` is a digest *reference*, requiring actual Room decision retrieval; bridge signer does not replace that authorization. `control_state_head` binds the full current BridgeControlState; direction values 1 outbound, 2 inbound, 3 both; state 1 active, 2 revoked.
4. **Policy byte shape:** mapping `allowed_event_classes` is a 1–18 set of existing event kind numbers; semantic declarations are 1–12 bounded enum values. Explicit boolean fields `relay_bridged`, `permit_fanout` and `require_interop_metadata` avoid an unbounded arbitrary policy map. **Conservative defaults** when constructing new mappings: no relay, no fanout, metadata preservation required; for outbound mapping the latter is mandatory. A signed mapping cannot itself opt the Room into relay/fanout.
5. **Nested provenance/lineage:** for interoperable key 14, the full `ForeignProvenanceBody` nested map has keys 0–9, while standalone type 57 wraps it under key 2. Likewise type 56 wraps `InteropTraceBody` under key 2; the two wrappers do not create an extra event kind. Foreign refs are opaque nonempty UTF-8 up to 512 bytes, namespaces up to 128 bytes, display hint up to 512 bytes and optional wall-clock source time a `uint` hint. Nine numeric fidelity flags include `origin_unverified`; `exact` is exclusive with all loss/unverified flags.
6. **Provenance MAC and disclosure:** `HMAC-SHA256` is the existing keyed-hash construction chosen over ambiguous byte concatenation, input a canonical three-element CBOR array, output exactly 32 bytes; the secret is a private Room-local 32-byte derived key **not in any public object**, and not a RoomID or BridgeID. **How that key is established, derived, rotated and shared across admitted members/bridges remains an external security-design/review obligation**; a public identifier as replacement is forbidden.
7. **Lineage limits:** 0–16 ordered traversed IDs, hop limit 1–16, `hop_count == len(path)`, no duplicate BridgeIDs or transformation IDs, and at most 16 transformation IDs (16–32 bytes each). A standalone terminal trace may represent hop_count equal to limit; an imported ConversationEvent at that limit is rejected, because it cannot be extended by another inbound bridge.
8. **Explicit link constraints:** proof methods enumerate signed challenge, OAuth-style proof, posted nonce, domain claim and bridge attestation; methods require external verifier evidence digest. `attesting_bridge_id` is required *only* for bridge-attestation proof. `expires_at` is mandatory, `not_before` optional; versions and predecessor hash enforce monotonic revocation and immutable `(AccountID,namespace,foreign actor)` identity. Signed controller authorization must be checked against its recorded exact AccountControlState head; format does **not** invent a new native account power.
9. **Cross-field validator cases:** mapping in/out direction, metadata-preserving requirement, control-head/adapters/features where supplied, unique endpoint/adapter namespaces, threshold bound, linked fingerprint/trace agreement, bridge-last-path/active endpoint and Room mapping where supplied, exclusive service vs bridge event overlay, required `foreign_actor_unverified` when no actor reference, and native event-body kind guards. The independently authenticated Room/Account/network state is an *external* check; the Python decoder must never imply that merely passing the wire checks grants admission, quorum or identity proof.
10. **Chain and collection caps:** `1..2^32−1` versions, genesis predecessor omitted, exact next version and current predecessor, no resurrection or endpoint tombstone loss, no version reuse. All bytes/array bounds are validator hard limits rather than descriptive hints; no generic free-form map for capability/policy/authentication bypass.

These choices must be assessed by an independent reviewer before product release, and any conflict with audited controls resolves in favor of the audited controls. **This ADR records an AI design decision and AI review; it does not satisfy independence.**
