# ADR-0083 — Messenger-baseline and community surfaces must be designed before engineering

## Status
Accepted — 2026-09-23

## Decision
Stickers/GIF-like animation, external content packs, bot/LLM agents, communities/spaces, channels/rooms, broadcast publication, topics/forums, roles/permissions, plugins and the first-release feature baseline are pre-engineering architecture questions because they affect canonical events, control authority, security boundaries or interoperability.

Broad direction is recorded, but exact semantics are deliberately queued rather than hardened accidentally during implementation.
