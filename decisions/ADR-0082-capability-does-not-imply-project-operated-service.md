# ADR-0082 — Supporting a capability does not make SeedLife its operator

## Status
Accepted — 2026-09-23

## Decision
OM may define interoperable capabilities for stickers/content packs, bots/LLMs, communities, broadcast publication, catalogs, entitlement proofs and future commerce without the OM Project hosting or operating those services.

Prefer direct sharing and replaceable providers. Commerce/payment/marketplace operation remains outside OM Core.
