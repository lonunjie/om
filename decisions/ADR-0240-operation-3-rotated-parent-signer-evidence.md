- **Answers:** which credential references verify the signatures of a signed parent whose signer rotated a credential (`CC3A-02`).
- **Relations:** Clarifies ADR-0236 §2–3; preserves ADR-0216 and the audited parent-authority rule of [`ACCOUNT_CONTINUITY_AND_CONTROL.md`](../docs/architecture/identity/ACCOUNT_CONTINUITY_AND_CONTROL.md) §4.1.

## Ruling

The signatures on an already accepted signed parent `P` authorize **the transition from its predecessor `Q` to `P`** and MUST be checked, when reverified, against **the DeviceCredential digest references committed in `Q`**, not the references newly committed in `P`. Signatures on the proposed operation-3 child `C`, and the origin/selected proofs for the operation-3 ceremony, use `P`'s committed credentials. A rotation by DeviceID `d` in `P` therefore has two distinct credential identities: `(d, H_old)` for verifying `P` against `Q`, and `(d, H_new)` for the operation-3 signatures/proofs against `P`. Neither is a duplicate of the other.

The selected controller has an independently and durably verified, unforked `P` **before** the preflight. Such a verified parent is an admissible trust anchor for verifying `P → C`; it need not replay `Q → P` merely because the preflight repeats the exact signed bytes of `P`. In this anchored mode the device MUST compare the supplied parent bytes/hash to its own accepted parent and verify `C` and all operation-3 proofs against `P`. It MUST NOT claim to have *newly* verified the signatures on `P` from the preflight alone.

If the selected controller *does* reverify the signed parent, the requisite predecessor authority context is either (a) its own previously validated and retained `Q` and its committed credential references, or (b) a contiguous, independently verified predecessor chain extending an earlier trusted local pin, carried by an existing authenticated sibling/ACS-evidence route. A preflight may transport missing canonical bytes, including `Q`, but a sender's authentication or its assertion that `Q` is authoritative is **not** itself a trusted predecessor pin. An arbitrary supplied `Q` or a credential body merely sharing a DeviceID does not pass. Check `H(DeviceCredential)` against `Q` for each signature on `P`, and against `P` for both operation-3 parties, and actually verify the applicable W0 and private-label signatures. If required authoritative context is missing, use the already verified `P` anchor; if `P` is not independently verified, refuse before voting. No fallback to a current key for a historical W0 signature.

## Exact private CDDL/semantic correction

No numeric field or maximum changes: ADR-0236 `op3-proposal-v2` key 10 remains `[2*18 bstr .cbor DeviceCredential]`. Its **exact union** is (i) the at most 16 signing credentials of the signed parent according to `Q`'s committed references, and (ii) the origin/selected credentials according to `P`'s committed references, deduplicated by the pair `(DeviceID, OM-DEVICE-CREDENTIAL-HASH-v1 digest value)`, not by DeviceID alone. This gives at most 18 distinct bodies. Canonical order is lexicographic `(DeviceID bytes, credential digest bytes)`; reject exact-pair duplicates, unsorted entries and every body not belonging to one of these two roles. The origin must know the genuine predecessor's credential references to construct this exact set; otherwise operation 3 is refused before voting, not sent with an invented or truncated union. The recipient unable to inspect `Q` may treat historical bodies as untrusted supplementary material while using its *already verified* `P` pin; it must not promote them into proof of `P`'s historical authorization.

The sentence in ADR-0236 §3 saying “sorted by DeviceID” and “no duplicate or unreferenced credential” must be **explicitly qualified** by this two-era rule, and its statement that the selected “must verify ... all credentials needed to verify a parent state” must distinguish durable prior validation from re-verification. This changes ADR-0236's gated private semantics, **not audited W0 text**; make the ADR-0236 pointer before enabling. No new CDDL key, W0 shape, domain label, KDF, AEAD or Home API field is required.

## Required vectors, conformance and metadata

- `CC3B-01`: fixed `Q → P` key rotation of the origin's DeviceID, signed `P` with `H_old`, `P` commits `H_new`; op-3 preflight key 10 includes **both** distinct credential bodies. Generate byte-exact preflight, the two existing private DS-SIG preimages, the selected request/W0 vote and origin reply/W0 vote. Check old signer against `Q`, both op-3 proofs and W0 child votes against `P`.
- `CC3B-02`: a selected device with only its durable verified `P` may accept an exact-match parent without rechecking signatures on `P`; a selected device without an authenticated `P` must refuse. An independently validated `Q` can verify `P`; a free-standing `Q` supplied by the origin cannot establish the parent.
- `CC3B-03`: wrong old hash, old/current-key swap, same-DeviceID deduplication, extra body, ambiguous order, nonexistent `Q`, stale/forked `P`, 19th distinct credential, and over-100,000-byte preflight all fail before an authorization vote or child adoption. Existing `CC3A-01` link and comparison code remain bit-identical.

The historical credential may expose a previous public key and key-rotation correlation **only to the authenticated selected controller**. Ceremony queue sizes/times and private-link candidate-hash exposure remain as in ADR-0236. This grants neither sender, old key nor Home new authority. No added Home-visible identifiers. Do not log credentials or proof plaintexts.

## Gate and audited-text check

Amend the **accepted but gated** ADR-0236 explanation and private fixture assertion; update machine traceability and run two independent real-signature codecs plus replay/fork tests. No operation-3 enablement or release is approved.
