# ADR-0218 — Calls wire freeze (CallSignal, type 44) and the constructions the design leaves to engineering

- **Status:** Accepted for W2 wire compatibility. Rulings 6 to 8 are operator rulings of 2026-11-18 (delegated from the constitution); the other rules are decided by delegation and amend no audited text. Specialist cryptographic review of rules 2 to 4 stays a release obligation (ADR-0217 point 4).
- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Scope:** engineering scope `calls` (ADR-0217), slice 0 of [`2026-11-18-calls-build-plan.md`](../docs/reports/2026-11-18-calls-build-plan.md). One-to-one calls only; group-call bodies are not frozen here.
- **Class:** `additive_noncritical_extension` (ADR-0165): one new object type, one new struct, four new enums, two new labels. No W0 object, label, enum value or frozen corpus changes.
- **Evidence:** [`call.cddl`](../spec/cddl/call.cddl); registry rows in [`om-object-types.json`](../spec/registries/om-object-types.json), [`om-numeric-fields.json`](../spec/registries/om-numeric-fields.json), [`om-domain-separation.json`](../spec/registries/om-domain-separation.json); 100 object vectors and 12 struct vectors in [`call-signal-vectors.json.gz`](../verification/vectors/call-signal-vectors.json.gz); [`call-wire`](../verification/prototypes/call-wire/run.sh) (generator, two strict codecs, two CDDL tools) and the product codec test `crates/om-codec/tests/call_signal_vectors.rs`.

## Decision

1. **One signed-free object.** `CallSignal` (object type 44, wave W2, scope `calls`) carries the eight logical kinds as `kind` 1 to 8 (`offer`, `answer`, `reject`, `end`, `ice_update`, `media_update`, `participant_update`, `key_epoch`). It is not signed: it exists only as the plaintext of a message sealed in an established one-to-one session, so the session authenticates the sender (ADR-0132 rule 2; the logical schema says signalling is encrypted inside the control channel). Fields: `call_session_id` (16 random bytes, unrelated to any AccountID, DeviceID or capability), `seq` (per-sender counter from 0, strictly increasing), and per kind `call_kind` (voice 1, video 2), `sdp` (1 to 16,384 bytes, all candidates gathered), `key_package`, `reason`, `media_flags` (bit 0 audio, bit 1 video). Object bound 32,768 bytes. Key 23 is the only extension point; keys 10 to 15 are held for group calls; there is **no time field**.
2. **Context binding.** The ratchet `ctx` of a sealed `CallSignal` is the label `OM-CALL-SIGNAL-CTX-v1`, so a call signal cannot be replayed as a chat message or the reverse.
3. **One-to-one media keys (resolves plan Q1).** The design says the secret derives from a "session exporter" bound to CallID and participants; the ratchet has none. The caller draws a fresh 256-bit `call_secret` per call and sends it in `CallKeyPackage` inside `offer`, to each callee device through that pair's own session. For each ordered (sender device, recipient device) pair: `base_key = HKDF-SHA256(salt = call_session_id, IKM = call_secret, info = OM-CBOR [OM-CALL-MEDIA-BASE-KDF-v1, sender_device_id, recipient_device_id, key_generation], 32)`; `key_generation` is 0 in v1 (the field exists so a rekey needs no wire change). Forward secrecy and sender authentication are those of the session; no new primitive.
4. **SFrame suite (resolves plan Q2).** One suite in v1: `call_media_suite` 1, the RFC 9605 AES-128-GCM with SHA-256 suite, run through WebCrypto in the client. The design names RFC 9605 as the framing; this is its mandatory suite, not an OM primitive. A new suite is a new enum value, additively.
5. **Reserved group kinds.** Kinds 7 and 8 are registered so no other meaning can take them; a one-to-one receiver refuses them and their bodies are **not** frozen. Their key-delivery channel needs the specialist review of R-343 first; group calls stay blocked on it. This leaves that part of the V-007 wire-freeze criterion open, as ADR-0217 point 4 records.
6. **Ring window (operator ruling, plan Q4).** 45 seconds, a profile constant counted by the receiver from its first sight of the offer. The Home deposit of an offer carries a 45-second lifetime so a stale offer is not delivered; an offer found later is a missed call, never rung. `end` (reason `canceled`) from the caller stops the ring.
7. **ICE servers (operator ruling, plan Q8).** STUN and TURN are available **only through the person's own Home**, as an optional Home feature that is off unless the Home advertises it. No third-party STUN or TURN is configured by default, so no IP address is revealed to a third party without the person's own Home choosing it. Without the feature a call works only where direct (host or peer-reflexive) candidates connect, and the UI says so plainly. The feature itself (`om.media.turn.v1`) is slice 7 and gets its own ADR.
8. **Per-contact calls (operator ruling, plan Q14).** An established, unblocked contact may ring by default; a message request or an unknown account never rings (the existing rule); contact details carry a simple switch "Allow calls" that the person can turn off per contact. The switch is local state (a Core preference), not wire.
9. **Behaviour the engineers implement (delegated, no wire).** One call per device; a second offer gets `reject` with reason `busy`. An offer goes to every callee device and the first `answer` wins; the caller then sends `end` with reason `answered_elsewhere` to the others. Candidates are gathered fully (up to 3 seconds) before an offer or answer is sent; `ice_update` is used only for an ICE restart. Three unanswered offers from one contact in ten minutes fold into one missed-call line with no ring. Signal admission: a signal that breaks a per-kind rule in the [`call.cddl`](../spec/cddl/call.cddl) header, an unknown or reserved kind, a repeated or lower `seq`, or a non-offer for an unknown call is dropped with no effect and no host call.

## Where did authority go?

Nowhere new. The session authenticates the sender; the contact relationship and the local switch decide who rings; the Home carries sealed bytes and, later, may supply ICE servers it advertises; it can start, join, key or end nothing.

## Consequences

- Engineers decode with the registry (`om-codec`) and never invent fields. The per-kind cross-field rules are Core's to enforce (slice 1) and are the semantic vectors of the corpus.
- Group-call bodies, the group key-delivery channel and the SFU message shapes remain open and unbuilt.
