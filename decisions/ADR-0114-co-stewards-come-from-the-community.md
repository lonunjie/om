# ADR-0114 — Co-stewards come from the community; not a first-release blocker

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Decided by:** operator ("a co-steward will come from the future users")

## Decision

Recruiting a co-steward is a stewardship milestone ([`PROJECT_STEWARDSHIP_AND_CONTINUITY.md`](../docs/constitution/PROJECT_STEWARDSHIP_AND_CONTINUITY.md) §10), not a W0 or
first-release blocker. Candidates come from early users, T1 hosts, public design reviewers and contributors. S2
public-production resilience claims still require multiple owners and a multi-party release root. Until then, the
compensating controls are required: the Protocol Continuity Bundle, rebuildable releases and the creator-disappearance
drill (V-017). R-083's honesty rule is unchanged.
