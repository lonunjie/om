# ADR-0081 — OM keeps six complementary meanings

## Status
Accepted — 2026-09-23

## Decision
OM intentionally means Open-source Messenger, OpenMessenger, OwnMessenger, OurMessenger, OneMessenger and OhMessenger. [`NAMING.md`](../docs/constitution/NAMING.md) defines the meanings.

They collectively require an open commons, independent implementations and agents, safe customization, strong shared-space capability, extensibility without fragmentation, and an approachable default product.
