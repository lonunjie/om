# ADR-0212 — Room admission evidence and frame retry

- **Status:** Accepted by operator delegation on 2026-11-18.
- **Class:** `additive_noncritical_extension` for the optional W1 lineage evidence. The retry rules define endpoint behavior inside existing expiry and authorization rules.
- **Amends:** No audited text, frozen W0 object, crypto profile or authorization rule.

## Design sources read

The group and account rules come from [Group Chat Architecture](../docs/architecture/groups/GROUP_CHAT_ARCHITECTURE.md), [Group Commit and State Convergence](../docs/architecture/groups/GROUP_COMMIT_AND_STATE_CONVERGENCE.md), [One-to-One Session Management](../docs/architecture/crypto/ONE_TO_ONE_SESSION_MANAGEMENT.md), [Account Continuity and Control](../docs/architecture/identity/ACCOUNT_CONTINUITY_AND_CONTROL.md), ADR-0120, ADR-0144, ADR-0173, ADR-0177, ADR-0187, ADR-0189, and ADR-0191. The release and discovery documents outside these decisions were outside the review for this ADR.

## Decision

1. An authenticated `group.accept` claiming a head ahead of the relationship pin waits as the exact sealed wrapper in the existing K7 queue. The receiver requests a refresh through the relationship and retries within the original lifetime. The poll cursor can advance to fetch the refresh while the cumulative relay acknowledgement waits for the held wrapper to be decided. Core releases the Accept only if the controller head matches the refreshed pin and all device and route checks succeed. A head behind the pin, a same-generation conflict, or invalid signatures against the matched pin cannot authorize the release.
2. Core may retain a Commit arriving on the route of a locally accepted invitation before the Welcome installs the Room, or on a Room route before its parent arrives. Both use the existing Room frame queue of eight entries and seven days, also subject to the wrapper's authenticated expiry. A retry runs the normal route, signature, ACS, intent and MLS checks. Buffer order grants no authority. Failed attempts leave the MLS and Room state together at their prior state.
3. A Welcome or Add Commit can include the signed ACS lineage as optional key 4 of `group-account-head`. The existing `credentials` list can supply historical credentials needed to verify the chain. The receiver verifies a chain from genesis under the ordinary ACS rules, requires its tip to match the supplied state and verifies the `AccountControlHead` against an active controller at that tip. The authenticated inviter or committer supplies the Room context for initial trust in the account's random identifier. Carried proofs never replace existing pins or create a contact binding. A member whose evidence is missing remains pending for the existing refresh path. Frames without a chain still decode, and accounts already proved by local pins need no chain from a frame.
4. An inviter sources evidence from its validated own state or an active relationship whose signed head matches the pinned state. First contact and K7 refresh provide the signed states. The proof is carried only for accounts named by the Welcome or Add Commit, inside the existing per-device seal, and remains bound by the existing single-frame size limit. A Welcome can omit the invitee's and inviter's accounts because the recipient already has those pins. Recovery successors still need the verifier's own recovery evidence under the recovery rules.
5. A messaging device can present a controller-signed head for its own validated tip. The existing authenticated enrollment payload or an authenticated sibling ACS update can supply it. A sibling update must match the receiver's current tip or extend it through the ordinary authorized successor rules. An unseen enrollment still requires its verified enrollment notice. No recovery link is inferred from a sibling assertion. An unsent Accept waits within its existing lifetime when the device lacks a matching controller head, and may ask its controller over the existing sibling session.

## Authority and evidence

Controller and parent-ACS signatures remain the sources of device authority. Room consent, MLS membership, and Commit ordering retain their existing gates. No directory, Home endpoint, signing key, or provider-visible field is added. Room recipients of the proof can see the historical ACS states and credential bodies needed to verify it. The single-frame bound still limits how much evidence can be delivered.

Wire shape and fallback are specified in [Group transport CDDL](../spec/cddl/group-transport.cddl). The vector corpus covers optional lineage evidence and its bounds. The Core loopbacks cover admission without a pairwise contact graph, advanced and corrupted heads, Commit and Welcome reordering, and restart. R-442, T-186 and D-223 trace the change.
