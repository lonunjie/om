# ADR-0261 — Authorize engineering scope `communities_publications` (W3), by explicit operator instruction

- **Status:** **Accepted** (operator, 2026-11-23, explicit instruction in chat; not delegation). Authorizing an engineering scope is a decision of the operator, not a review result and not a delegation. The operator's instruction was conditional: authorize each remaining scope that is part of the design and aligns with the constitution. This ADR records that check for this scope. The ADR-0130 guard required the `publication` conformance suite to be specified first; it now is (cases PUB-01 to PUB-14 in the Spaces and Publication document, restating existing rules and adding none).
- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Authorizes:** engineering scope `communities_publications` only. No other scope changes state through this ADR.
- **Depends on:** the scopes named in `depends_on` for `communities_publications` in the repository contract, already authorized.
- **Waives:** the **pre-engineering** phase of gate V-008 for engineering purposes only. Its pre-release phase is untouched and stays open.

## Context

`communities_publications` (W3) is part of the complete, audited design and its scope contract exists in [`engineering_scope_contracts.json`](../machine/engineering_scope_contracts.json). Constitution check: C-12 (principals hold authority, never providers or hosts) and C-01 (no required OM operator, so no project-run moderation). The design queue has no open blocking item for it. The unlock contract ([`RELEASE_WAVES_AND_SCOPED_ENGINEERING_UNLOCK.md`](../docs/process/RELEASE_WAVES_AND_SCOPED_ENGINEERING_UNLOCK.md) section 5) otherwise requires every gate that lists the scope to have `pre_engineering_status` passed or waived by an ADR. Gate V-008 still has criteria open: independent reviewer evidence on the authority model, and the two-codec agreement on the frozen Space and Publication wire (the formal model, incarnation and ceiling checks, delegated-intent checks and invite-lapse checks are met; V-016 pre-engineering has passed; the `publication` conformance suite is specified as PUB-01 to PUB-14). The operator may choose to authorize engineering now and keep that evidence as a release obligation. This is the same shape as ADR-0250, ADR-0217 and ADR-0183.

## Decision

1. **`communities_publications` is authorized**, by explicit operator instruction. Engineers follow [`ENGINEER_START_HERE.md`](../docs/process/ENGINEER_START_HERE.md), the `communities_publications` entry of [`engineering_scope_contracts.json`](../machine/engineering_scope_contracts.json) and the design gate in [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).
2. **What the scope covers.** Spaces, topics, Publications and delegated moderation on top of the authorized `groups` core: `SpaceControlState`, `SpaceDelegatedIntent`, `PublicationControlState`, `PublicationEvent`, `topic.*` events and `moderation.redact`. Sources: [`SPACES_ROOMS_COMMUNITIES_AND_PUBLICATION.md`](../docs/architecture/groups/SPACES_ROOMS_COMMUNITIES_AND_PUBLICATION.md), [`EVENT_MODEL.md`](../docs/architecture/events/EVENT_MODEL.md).
   - **Not covered:** massive private broadcast cryptography (none in V1), any hosted community directory or feed, any operator-held moderation authority. Each needs its own ADR.
3. **Gate V-008 pre-engineering is waived by this ADR, for engineering only.**
   - *Risk accepted:* Space and Publication authority code can be written before independent review of the authority model, so a grant or ceiling rule chosen during engineering could be found wrong, and an authority-fork rule could be implemented ahead of its review.
   - *Compensating controls:* (a) the wire objects are already frozen as [`spec/cddl/space-publication.cddl`](../spec/cddl/space-publication.cddl) (ADR-0160), so the first build slice confirms that freeze with two independently written codecs agreeing on its positive and negative vectors before any other product code, and where it must change the freeze it does so (CDDL, registered numeric fields and domain-separation labels, positive and negative vectors, two independently written codecs agreeing) before any other product code, by the V-005 method, additively and under ADR-0165; (b) the feature is built behind a capability flag that is off in every release until the release evidence below is in, and no production build offers it; (c) every construction the design leaves open is written in an ADR by delegation that does not amend audited text, and where the design is silent the engineer writes the question in the scope's plan and stops; (d) no wire requirement is added to an earlier wave beyond the reserved extension and criticality points already frozen in V1 semantics (RELEASE_WAVES rule 1); (e) authority forks are frozen until authenticated resolution, never resolved by timestamp or provider arrival order; (f) Space or Publication authority never silently becomes account authority or universal Room owner authority; moderation decisions change no Room state until committed; (g) the `publication` conformance suite cases PUB-01 to PUB-14 are the first tests written.
4. **Still required BEFORE RELEASE, not before engineering.** the model-derived vectors replayed by an independent implementation, plus the independent review; any release offering Spaces or Publications, declaring `communities_publications`, or claiming moderation guarantees needs them. `om-open-beta-1` does not list `communities_publications` and stays as it is. Authorization is not release: nothing here edits a public release declaration.
5. **Constraints.** No new cryptographic primitive and no weakened floor (C-20); no mandatory third-party service and no project-operated service for this scope (C-01); no design change. Where the design is silent the engineer writes the question in the scope's plan and stops, or an ADR by delegation records a construction that does not amend audited text. The first deliverable for the scope is a build plan that states which design documents were read.
6. **Where did authority go?** Granted only by the committed Space or Room control state; a Space may constrain its own Rooms but never becomes account or universal authority.

## Consequences

- The review basis becomes the operator's decision, not independent review. The open independence criteria of V-008 move to the release obligations in point 4.
- The first slice is design-bound specification and test work (wire freeze and the evidence harness named above); no feature code is reachable in any build until the capability flag is turned on by a later, evidenced decision.
